Lantern Rite is an original card duel on a lantern-lit river: play one card a turn into one of three barges, light two barges to win the round, and take three lanterns — or spend your rival's spirit — to win the match. Four suits told apart by colour and shape, harmony and run bonuses, and three trick cards that copy, blow away, and kindle. Rin, the rival keeper, shows her tell before she plays, so a keeper watching closely can contest the barge she eyes.
